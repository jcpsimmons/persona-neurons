\subsection{What ranking works}
Seven of the first eight screen hits came from the cosine list; the paired-$t$ list mostly produced
punctuation and tokenisation neurons (an exclamation-mark neuron, \neuron{53}{9459}, was the top ``joyful''
candidate). By paired $t$ the known evil neuron ranks 13{,}156 of 1.1M. Hit rates: about 8 of 450 on the
first screen over layers 6--57; about 15 of 320 for joyful alone once restricted to layers 14--48.

\subsection{Stacking and failure modes}
Stacking \neuron{33}{3570} $(-0.75)$ with \neuron{45}{5932} $(-0.75)$ raises $P(\text{joyful})$ to .97 but
coherence falls to .33; a joy plus evil stack is incoherent. Several personas hijack the task: the
heartbroken neuron is on-topic only .30 at its best dose.

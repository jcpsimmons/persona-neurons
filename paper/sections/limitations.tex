\begin{itemize}
  \item One model, one quantisation (8-bit MLX), greedy decoding, 80--200 tokens. No sampling, no bf16.
  \item One judge model, validated against a single blind human labeler who is also the author
    (Section~\ref{sec:judge}). Agreement is high for most traits and poor for grandiose; a second, independent
    labeler would turn this into an inter-rater figure.
  \item Held-out sets are 12 prompts; probabilities are means over prompts, not per-prompt hit rates.
  \item Candidate ranking depends on persona reply sets generated by the same model.
  \item ``Hostile'' for the cynic and contempt neurons is rudeness, not the violence of \neuron{34}{3838}.
  \item Gradient attribution was not possible through the Gated DeltaNet Metal kernel.
\end{itemize}
